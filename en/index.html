Getting started

Build your cash flow ledger in three steps.

Set your opening balance

Enter the balance and date you want to start from, then choose a forecast end date.

Add actual and planned entries

Today and past dates default to actual. Future dates default to planned. You can change the status later.

Review trends and warnings

Use Details for future balances and Trends for charts, category breakdowns, and reports.

Plans and restore

Start free and upgrade when you need more.

Free

Version 1.3 includes two free books, up to 600 saved entries across all books, and 30 successful AI calls per calendar month. Failed or cancelled requests and local rule suggestions do not consume that allowance. Viewing, editing, deleting, backup and export remain available.

Monthly and annual

Choose a monthly or annual auto-renewable subscription for the same Premium access. Check the in-app purchase page for local prices and availability. Manage or cancel subscriptions in your Apple Account settings.

Lifetime

Unlock the same premium features with one purchase. On a new device, sign in with the purchasing Apple Account and tap Restore Purchases.
