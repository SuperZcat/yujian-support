Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 27, 2026

Forecash Planner respects your privacy. This policy explains how the Forecash Planner app handles data. Forecash Planner does not require an account, display ads, or use third-party advertising trackers or behavioral analytics.

1. Data stored on your device

Transactions, categories, settings, attachments, analysis results, and related ledger data are stored on your device by default. Forecash Planner does not provide iCloud sync and does not automatically upload this content to a developer server. You may export backups or Excel files, which you control and share at your discretion.

2. On-device AI

On-device AI does not run by default. After you enable the relevant feature and request it, Forecash Planner may download the Qwen3.5-4B-4bit model over Wi-Fi on first use or after a model update. Downloading over cellular data requires separate confirmation. The app displays the model version and download size. The system may continue a model-file download after you switch apps, but categorization and analysis run only while the app is in the foreground. Ledger entries, descriptions, amounts, and categories are not sent to the hosting service. Download requests may expose ordinary network information, such as your IP address, to the provider. The app attempts to release model resources after completion or cancellation; the system may take time to reclaim memory. Completed reports stay on your device until replaced by a newly completed report or removed through reset. Local rules and basic statistics remain available if device memory is insufficient. AI output is for reference, not professional financial advice.

3. Network features

The following features connect to the internet when needed, without sending ledger entries:

  • Apple StoreKit for product information, purchases, and purchase restoration.
  • Public foreign-exchange rates from Bank of China.
  • Downloading the optional on-device AI model.
  • Fetching fixed-source public financial education when you request it.

3a. Optional city and consumer-price references

Off by default. When you enable public-price lookup and start an analysis, mainland China first uses the National Bureau of Statistics' public national monthly consumer-price tables (www.stats.gov.cn), plus monthly consumer-price index packets covering 31 provincial-level areas and 36 cities (data.stats.gov.cn). Other regions, or an unavailable Chinese monthly lookup, use the World Bank country directory and a worldwide inflation dataset for the latest three completed years. Japan additionally uses e-Stat's monthly directory and nationwide city item-price table. Country, province and city selection from public datasets happens on your device, not in request parameters. City and consent preferences stay on the device and are not transferred with ledger backups. Requests contain no transactions, merchants, amounts, notes, custom categories or user identifiers. The app does not obtain your location. Expanded sources require you to save network consent again. Personal local analysis continues when data is unavailable, outdated or unreliable.

Providers may receive your IP address, request time and connection information; the provider selected may also suggest a broad country context. This is not anonymous networking. Reference-data requests disable cookies, credential storage and persistent network caching, but the app does not control provider logs or retention. Opening a source webpage separately is subject to your browser and that website's privacy practices. See e-Stat's privacy policy and terms, China's statistical-data terms, and the World Bank's data and license and privacy notice.

Reports retain sources, geographic scope, data periods and retrieval times locally. In the Trends screen, tap the “Analysis notes and sources” information button below the analysis report to view its basis and applicable reference sources. The latest published statistics are not live product quotes; the 36-city figures describe consumer-price index changes, not average product prices in each city. City, provincial or national price changes provide background for on-device analysis, not personal-spending changes, and are not subtracted from a recent month's spending growth. Similar-city references identify the actual city and are not treated as local averages or proof of overspending when quantities or specifications are unknown. Turning the feature off stops future requests but retains old reports. Clearing data or resetting settings deletes reports; resetting settings also clears city and network consent.

4. Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and lifetime purchases. The developer does not receive your card number or full payment details. Forecash Planner uses only Apple-verified transaction status to determine premium access.

5. Feedback and diagnostics

Feedback is never sent automatically. When you choose to send feedback, Forecash Planner opens a system email draft that may contain your text, selected screenshots, and an optional diagnostic log. The log records only predefined feature names, outcomes, timestamps, and durations. On-device AI events may also include physical, available, and required device memory in MiB, plus categories and counts of analysis validation outcomes. It does not contain ledger entries, prompts, or model output. Review the draft before sending. Sent emails are handled by the email provider and retained only as needed to reply, investigate issues, and improve the product. You may contact us to request deletion.

6. Notifications, authentication, and permissions

With permission, Forecash Planner can schedule local repayment or receivable reminders and use Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode to protect local reset actions. iOS performs authentication, and Forecash Planner does not access or store biometric data. Selected files and photos are used only for actions you initiate.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall the app. Feedback diagnostic events are limited to 7 days and 100 events. Use Settings → Security & Local Reset to clear data or reset settings.

8. Third-party services

Network features may access Apple, Bank of China, Hugging Face, the U.S. Consumer Financial Protection Bureau, and the optional reference providers above: China's National Bureau of Statistics, the World Bank and e-Stat. They process network requests under their own privacy policies. Forecash Planner does not sell ledger data to these services.

9. Children's privacy

Forecash Planner is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us if you believe a child submitted personal information through feedback.

10. Policy changes

If our data handling changes materially, we will update the effective date on this page and, where appropriate, explain the change in the app or App Store listing.
