Privacy Policy

Privacy Policy

Effective September 21, 2026

Yujian respects your privacy. This policy explains how the Yujian app handles data. Yujian does not require an account, display ads, or use third-party advertising trackers or behavioral analytics.

1. Data stored on your device

Transactions, categories, settings, attachments, analysis results, and related ledger data are stored on your device by default. Yujian does not provide iCloud sync and does not automatically upload this content to a developer server. You may export backups or Excel files, which you control and share at your discretion.

2. On-device AI

On-device AI does not run by default. The first time you use it, Yujian downloads the Qwen3.5-2B-4bit model from a model hosting service after your confirmation. Categorization and analysis run on your device. Ledger content is not sent to the model hosting service. The download request may expose ordinary network information, such as your IP address, to the provider.

3. Network features

The following features connect to the internet when needed, without sending ledger entries:

  • Apple StoreKit for product information, purchases, and purchase restoration.
  • Public foreign-exchange rates from Bank of China.
  • Downloading the optional on-device AI model.
  • Fetching fixed-source public financial education when you request it.

4. Purchases

Apple processes subscriptions and lifetime purchases. The developer does not receive your card number or full payment details. Yujian uses only Apple-verified transaction status to determine premium access.

5. Feedback and diagnostics

Feedback is never sent automatically. When you choose to send feedback, Yujian opens a system email draft that may contain your text, selected screenshots, and an optional diagnostic log. The log contains only predefined feature names, outcomes, timestamps, and durations, not ledger content. Review the draft before sending. Sent emails are handled by the email provider and retained only as needed to reply, investigate issues, and improve the product. You may contact us to request deletion.

6. Notifications, authentication, and permissions

With permission, Yujian can schedule local repayment or receivable reminders and use Face ID, Touch ID, or the device passcode to protect local reset actions. iOS performs authentication, and Yujian does not access or store biometric data. Selected files and photos are used only for actions you initiate.

7. Retention and deletion

Local data remains on the device until you delete it, reset the app, or uninstall the app. Feedback diagnostic events are limited to 7 days and 100 events. Use Settings → Security & Local Reset to clear data or reset settings.

8. Third-party services

Network features may access services from Apple, Bank of China, Hugging Face, and the U.S. Consumer Financial Protection Bureau. They process network requests under their own privacy policies. Yujian does not sell ledger data to these services.

9. Children's privacy

Yujian is not directed to children and does not knowingly collect children's personal information. Contact us if you believe a child submitted personal information through feedback.

10. Policy changes

If our data handling changes materially, we will update the effective date on this page and, where appropriate, explain the change in the app or App Store listing.

11. Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to tztaozhang@outlook.com.
